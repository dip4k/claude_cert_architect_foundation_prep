OVERVIEW
--------
Dataset: 500 customers
Overall Churn Rate: 38.6% (193 churned customers)
Analysis Method: Statistical Testing + Random Forest Feature Importance

================================================================================
TOP 8 CHURN DRIVERS (RANKED)
================================================================================

1. TOTAL VIEWING HOURS (22.7% importance) ⭐⭐⭐
   • Churned: 66.6 hrs vs Not Churned: 83.2 hrs (-20%)
   • Statistical significance: p < 0.000001
   • ACTION: Target users with <70 hrs/month for re-engagement

2. AVERAGE SESSION DURATION (21.1% importance) ⭐⭐⭐
   • Churned: 49.4 min vs Not Churned: 57.8 min (-14%)
   • Statistical significance: p < 0.000001
   • ACTION: Improve content stickiness and autoplay features

3. UNIQUE TITLES WATCHED (16.1% importance) ⭐⭐⭐
   • Churned: 19.5 titles vs Not Churned: 23.7 titles (-18%)
   • Statistical significance: p < 0.000001
   • ACTION: Promote content discovery and exploration

4. BINGE WATCHING SESSIONS (11.6% importance) ⭐⭐
   • Churned: 6.2 sessions vs Not Churned: 7.7 sessions (-20%)
   • Statistical significance: p < 0.000001
   • ACTION: Promote binge-worthy series and complete season releases

5. TOP GENRE (11.4% importance) ⭐⭐
   • Horror & Thriller fans have highest churn (52% & 48%)
   • Documentary & Comedy fans have lowest churn (26% & 33%)
   • ACTION: Strengthen Horror/Thriller content library

6. CUSTOMER SERVICE INTERACTIONS (9.7% importance) ⚠️ WARNING SIGN
   • Churned: 3.2 vs Not Churned: 2.5 (+28%)
   • This is the ONLY metric higher for churned customers
   • Statistical significance: p < 0.000001
   • ACTION: Flag users with 4+ interactions for immediate retention efforts

7. SUBSCRIPTION TIER (3.4% importance) ⭐
   • Premium: 24% churn | Standard: 40% | Basic: 44%
   • Statistical significance: p = 0.009
   • ACTION: Incentivize upgrades to Premium tier

8. MONTHLY COST (4.1% importance)
   • Churned: $11.18 vs Not Churned: $12.11 (-8%)
   • Statistical significance: p = 0.005
   • ACTION: Focus on value delivery over price competition

================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

✓ ENGAGEMENT IS EVERYTHING
  Low engagement (viewing hours, session duration, content exploration) 
  is the PRIMARY driver of churn, not price or service issues.

✓ CUSTOMER SERVICE = RED FLAG
  High CS interactions are a leading indicator of churn. Users with 4+
  interactions need proactive retention intervention.

✓ PREMIUM USERS STAY LONGER
  Premium tier has 45% lower churn than Basic tier, suggesting value
  and features drive retention more than price.

✓ RISK SEGMENTATION WORKS
  - High Risk (low engagement): 57% churn rate
  - Medium Risk: 37% churn rate
  - Low Risk (high engagement): 19% churn rate
